TrueMile EV — Delete Your Account & Data

TrueMile EV is built and operated by Gate Engineering LLC. This page explains how to delete your account and data, exactly what is deleted, and the small vehicle-only record that can be retained. The full privacy policy is at gateeng.com/truemile-privacy.

Delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open TrueMile EV and tap the Settings gear.
  2. Tap My Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Or delete it on the web

  1. Sign in at gateeng.com/truemileev/app.html with the same account.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account…, type the confirmation word, and confirm.

Deletion runs immediately and is permanent — there is no recovery or grace period. Our database host keeps backups for up to 7 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup until that backup rolls off. We keep only the deleted account’s random ID and the time of deletion, for 35 days, so that if we ever had to restore a backup we could delete the account again.

Have a Pro or Max subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it first in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or it will keep renewing.

Can't sign in anywhere?

Email support@gateeng.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my TrueMile EV account". The deletion is performed for you, with a confirmation reply when done.

What is deleted, and what can be retained

Deleted immediately with your accountRetained
Your sign-in identity (email, name, Google sign-in details) and account ID · sign-in records (IP address and device type) · every trip and its GPS route · charging sessions and their locations, including your home charger location · all prices, costs, and rates you entered · your notes · saved places · settings, saved trailers, and paired-adapter details · generated mileage reports · subscription records held by the app A vehicle service history, keyed to the vehicle's VIN — like a service book that follows the car to its next owner: vehicle specs and the details read from the door-jamb label, day-level charging facts, battery state-of-health, diagnostic codes, and tire records. It holds no name, email, account, locations, routes, prices, or notes, but it is not anonymous: a VIN identifies one vehicle, and others can link a VIN to its registered owner. Don't want it kept? Email us the VIN and we delete it once your account deletion has run (the record is only created then, so a request sent before you delete is carried out after the deletion has run).

A one-way code computed with a secret key from the VIN and adapter address (it contains neither, and cannot be turned back into them without that key), kept up to 90 days so the introductory period can't be restarted on the same vehicle.

Community price contributions (under a coded identifier, never your email, account ID or VIN; expire automatically within 14 days), crash reports (identified only by a random install ID, never your account; deleted after 90 days), and our database host's request logs (each request's IP address and web address, which can include the VIN or a record's ID; kept up to 7 days).

Data on the phone itself (local database, settings, logs) is removed by uninstalling the app.

Delete some data without closing your account

  • Individual trips: open the trip in the app and delete it — the cloud copy is removed as well.
  • Community price contributions: turn off in Settings → My Account at any time (past contributions carry only a coded identifier and expire on their own within 14 days).
  • Anything else (for example a specific charging session): email support@gateeng.com from your account email and it is removed for you.
